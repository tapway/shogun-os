Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2, 2026

1. Purpose

This Privacy Policy explains how Shogun OS collects, uses, and protects information when you use our internal finance dashboard connected to QuickBooks Online.

2. Information We Collect

Our application accesses your QuickBooks Online account to retrieve financial data including:

  • Financial transactions (invoices, bills, payments)
  • Account balances and financial statements
  • Customer and vendor information
  • Chart of accounts

3. How We Use Information

All data is used solely for:

  • Displaying financial dashboards and reports
  • Generating internal business analytics
  • Supporting financial decision-making

4. Data Storage & Security

  • Data is stored locally within our organization's infrastructure
  • Access is restricted to authorized personnel only
  • We implement industry-standard security measures
  • No data is sold or shared with third parties

5. QuickBooks Online Integration

Our application connects to QuickBooks Online via Intuit's official API. All data access follows Intuit's Developer Terms of Service and requires explicit user authorization through OAuth 2.0.

6. Internal Use Only

This application is designed for internal company use only. It is not intended for public distribution or external customer access.

7. Your Rights

Authorized users may:

  • Disconnect QuickBooks integration at any time
  • Request deletion of cached financial data
  • Contact administrators for data access requests

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Continued use of the application after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact Us

For questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact your system administrator or IT department.
