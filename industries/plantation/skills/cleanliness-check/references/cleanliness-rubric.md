# Cleanliness Rubric

## Cleanliness Scale

| Rating | Floor | Walls | Bedding | Surfaces |
|--------|-------|-------|---------|----------|
| Good | Swept/mopped, no stains | Clean, no marks | Fresh sheets, clean | Wiped, no dust |
| Moderate | Needs sweeping | Minor marks | Sheets present, need changing | Slight dust |
| Poor | Visible dirt/debris | Stains/marks | Dirty or no sheets | Thick dust/stains |

## Surface Assessment

### Floor
- Look for: dirt, debris, trash, stains, spillage
- Rating: clean (swept/mopped) / needs sweeping (visible dust) / dirty (debris/trash)
- If floor is not in frame: "not visible"

### Walls
- Look for: marks, stains, handprints, mold, cobwebs
- Rating: clean / marks (minor) / stains (visible dirt)
- Note: wall **cracks** are structural → `site-condition-check`, not here

### Bedding
- Look for: sheets, pillowcases, blanket condition
- Rating: fresh & clean / present, needs changing (wrinkled, dusty) / dirty / absent
- If no bed in frame: "not visible"

### Surfaces (tables, shelves, lockers)
- Look for: dust, clutter, stains on surfaces
- Rating: clean / dusty / dirty

## Overall Rating

| Rating | Criteria |
|--------|---------|
| Good | All surfaces clean, no issues |
| Moderate | 1-2 surfaces need attention |
| Poor | 3+ surfaces dirty or major issues |

## Notes

- Cleanliness ≠ structural condition. Stains on walls = cleanliness issue. Cracks in walls = structural issue.
- If a surface is not visible in the photo, rate it "not visible" — do not guess.
- Take the worst rating across multiple photos of the same surface.
