## Key Observations

1. **Shared departments are identical** across all 3 industries — HR, Finance, Procurement, CRM, Marketing, Compliance, Support, Coding. Same skills, same personas, same ports.

2. **Each industry has unique departments** that don't exist in other industries:
   - General: Projects + Product (service delivery + product management)
   - Manufacturing: Production + Quality + Maintenance + Warehouse + HSE (factory ops)
   - Retail: Stores + Merchandising + E-commerce + CRM/Loyalty + Supply Chain + VM (omnichannel)

3. **Some skills are shared WITHIN an industry** across departments:
   - Manufacturing: `erp-connector` in Production + Quality + Warehouse; `mes-connector` in Production + Maintenance
   - Retail: `store-replenishment` in Stores + Supply Chain; `promo-planning` in Merchandising + Visual Merchandising

4. **E-commerce is a cross-industry add-on** — the new plan makes it available to ALL industries, not just Retail. A manufacturer or services company can add E-commerce to sell direct on Shopee/Lazada/TikTok/Website.

5. **Marketing and Compliance are shared but get e-commerce add-on skills** — when E-commerce is selected, Marketing gets 6 new skills (promo, content, banner) and Compliance gets 2 new skills (approval gate, audit log). Without E-commerce, they stay at their base skill set.

6. **Retail's E-commerce department already has 3 existing skills** (`ecommerce-listing`, `ecommerce-order-management`, `marketplace-analytics`) — the 17 new skills are ADDED to these, not replacing them.
